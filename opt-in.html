Aspire Workforce SMS Opt-In (Paper Form)

This page documents how subscribers provide consent to receive operational SMS messages from Aspire Workforce.

How users opt in

Users opt in during application, onboarding, or assignment setup with staff. The subscriber provides their mobile number and gives explicit consent via a paper onboarding SMS consent form (checkbox + signature). Consent is recorded and retained in our internal system.

Disclosures shown to subscribers

  • Program name: Aspire Workforce
  • Message types: job/shift offers, assignment confirmations, schedule reminders, time-sensitive updates (changes/cancellations), and support-related messages
  • Message frequency: varies
  • Msg & data rates: may apply
  • Opt-out: Reply STOP to opt out
  • Help: Reply HELP for help or email support@evergrowthdigitalcreations.com
